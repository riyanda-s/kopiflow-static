# Commercial packages and handover

These are suggested **one-time freelance implementation packages**, not menu products. They are not displayed or sold on the coffee shop website. Confirm real effort and scope in writing before accepting a job; estimates below assume this working template, complete client content and one location.

| Package | Fee | Suggested scope | Estimated focused effort |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | Rp499.000 | Existing four-page layout; logo/colors/contact replacement; up to 20 supplied products; existing cart/WhatsApp/QR; one revision round; one static deployment and brief handover. | 4–6 hours |
| Standard | Rp799.000 | Basic plus up to 35 supplied products, richer option/add-on setup, approved story/gallery, image optimization, two revision rounds, domain connection and documented QA. | 7–10 hours |
| Premium | Rp1.299.000 | Standard plus up to 50 supplied products, more extensive copy/layout refinement within the existing page scope, three revision rounds, printable QR proof, launch walkthrough and agreed post-launch fixes. | 11–16 hours |

The underlying template includes all functional features for every package. Fees cover configuration, content work and service effort. New functionality, extra pages, photography, translation, logo design, domain fees, paid hosting, legal review, ongoing content work and major redesign are separately quoted. Clarify taxes, revision definitions, turnaround and support duration in the agreement. Avoid unlimited revisions or lifetime maintenance promises.

## Delivery inventory

- Customer-owned repository, complete source, lockfile and configuration.
- Hosting project, final canonical domain, DNS record inventory and renewal owner.
- Approved product/option price sheet, imagery and third-party licensing notes.
- `README.md`, configuration guide, Cloudflare deployment guide and actual QA results.
- Production `dist/` archive if agreed; keep the associated source commit for reproducibility.
- Branded QR SVG and a scanned printed proof pointing at the final menu URL.
- Current browser/device and Lighthouse test results, including any unmet targets.

## Live acceptance walkthrough

1. Confirm every price, available product, add-on, address and contact channel.
2. Customize two different drinks, change quantities and compare estimates manually.
3. Prepare WhatsApp message, check recipient and send only with explicit authorization. Verify the shop responds manually. Do not imply automated order receipt/payment.
4. Show local cart persistence, clearing, privacy statement and unavailable products.
5. Scan QR from two phones; demonstrate downloadable SVG and printing.
6. Explain menu changes require rebuilding/deploying; a website cannot reflect unreported sold-out items.

## Transfer and support

Use account invitations/ownership transfer, not shared passwords. Customer controls domain renewals and hosting accounts. Remove temporary access after agreed support ends. Keep secrets out of Git; none are needed by this template. Retain a rollback release with the customer’s approval.

Optional paid maintenance can cover a defined number of content updates per month, stock/price revisions, dependency review, backups of source and periodic QA. Set response windows, emergency scope and fees explicitly. Hosting free tiers are subject to provider limits and terms; do not promise permanently free or unlimited service.
